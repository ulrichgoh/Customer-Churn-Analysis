# **Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard**

Interactive Power BI dashboard designed to monitor customer churn, retention, and customer behavior.

## **Project overview**

This project analyzes customer data to identify churn patterns, understand the main factors influencing customer attrition, and measure the business impact of customer loss. The solution supports data-driven decision-making through clear KPIs, interactive filters, and detailed visualizations.

## **What the dashboard shows**

* **Churn rate** and total customers at a glance
* **Customer churn patterns** by contract type, tenure, payment method, and customer demographics
* **Financial impact** through monthly charges, total revenue, and revenue at risk
* **Customer segmentation** based on churn status, tenure, services, and customer profile
* **Interactive filtering** by contract, payment method, customer status, tenure, and service type

## **Tools**

* **Power BI** — data modeling and interactive dashboard design
* **DAX** — KPI and business metric calculations
* **Power Query** — data cleaning, preparation, and transformation

## **Outcome**

The dashboard transforms customer data into actionable insights, making it easier to identify customers at risk of churn, understand retention challenges, prioritize customer retention strategies, and communicate key churn trends to stakeholders.
